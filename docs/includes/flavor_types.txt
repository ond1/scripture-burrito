Burritos currently come in four **flavors**:

* **Text Translation** — a Scripture text in USFM, USX, or USJ format
* **Audio Translation** — a recorded audio translation
* **Alignment** — word-level alignment between two texts, such as a translation and a source language
* **Wrapper** — groups related burritos together, such as a text and audio burrito for the same translation

Flavors are grouped into **flavor types** based on their relationship to Scripture (Scripture, Gloss, Parascriptural, or Peripheral). You can create your own nonstandard flavors using the ``x-`` prefix; see :ref:`extending_scripture_burrito`.
